# Aetherion Online — Milestone 13: Quests and Faction Reputation

Version **0.13.0-alpha**.

## 1. Honest summary

- **Quests and reputation work end to end.** A player accepts a quest from a town's lorekeeper, progress comes only from events the server resolved (kills it credited, dungeon clears, the bag, discoveries), the quest is turned in at the same lorekeeper for experience, gold, items and reputation, and all of it is stored in PostgreSQL. Shown by unit, integration and browser tests.
- **48 quests** across six regions, four dungeons and two world bosses. They are generated from the game's own data (each region's monsters, materials, caches and elite), each with its own name and text. They are serviceable, not hand-crafted story content: the "main quest structure for future use" is a six-step chain, one step per region.
- **Factions are database records.** The six factions live in a `factions` table; the server loads them at start, validates them and sends them to clients. A row added there appears in the game without a code change (tested).
- **Not judged by a person:** balance of experience and reputation rewards, and whether the quest text reads well.

## 2. What the player gets

- **Quest givers:** the lorekeeper in every town (talk with **F**). The dialog lists the quests on offer (with the reason if one is not available yet), active quests from that town, and **Turn in** when one is complete.
- **Quest log (L):** active quests with objectives, progress, rewards and **Abandon** (with confirmation); **History** (most recent 50 completions); **Factions** (standing with each).
- **Tracker:** up to four active quests on screen, with live progress; it says where to return when a quest is complete.
- **Sounds:** accepting and completing a quest now play their sounds (they were waiting for this milestone in M10).

## 3. Quest types

| Type | Per | Example | Repeat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main | region (6), each requires the previous | The Crater Watch: defeat Old Tusk and 6 Mosslings | once |
| Side | region | Trouble on the Roads: two monster types | once |
| Daily | region | Eldoria Patrol: 20 monsters anywhere in the region | once per UTC day |
| Repeatable hunting | region | Bounty: 10 of one monster | always |
| Collection | region | 5 of the region's material, handed over at turn-in | always |
| Exploration | region | find both hidden caches of the region | once |
| Faction | region, needs Friendly | 15 monsters including 2 of one type | always |
| Dungeon | dungeon (4) | clear the dungeon on any difficulty | always |
| Boss | world boss (2) | take part in defeating it | always |

**Rules:** level requirement; prerequisites; faction-standing requirement; at most 15 active quests; collection items are taken at turn-in; the reward must fit in the bag before anything is taken. Daily quests come back at midnight UTC. Nothing limits how often repeatable quests are done, in keeping with the project's no-energy, no-lockout rule (its test still passes).

## 4. Factions and reputation

Levels: **Neutral** 0, **Friendly** 500, **Honored** 1,500, **Revered** 4,000, **Exalted** 9,000 (cap 12,000). Reputation comes from quests of the faction's region and is stored per character.

- **Announcements:** reaching a level is announced to everyone nearby ("X is now Honored with The Cinder Compact").
- **Rewards:** each level reached gives a one-time gift (potions, then Random, Advanced and Rare Rune Scrolls).
- **Unlocks:** faction quests from Friendly; faction shop items by level.
- **Faction shops** at the faction seat's lorekeeper: potions and scrolls from Neutral/Friendly, a pet whistle from Honored, a mount from Revered or Exalted.

**Adding a faction:** insert a row into `factions` (id, name, seat, region, creed, about, shop as `[[item, price, level], ...]`). Rows naming a region without a lorekeeper are skipped with a warning; shop lines naming unknown items or bad prices are dropped. Quests that reward a new faction are added in `shared/src/quests.ts`.

The server side of M14 (guilds, auction house, bank) and M15 (PvP, duels, arena, pets, mounts) was written in this pass, and its tables exist.
