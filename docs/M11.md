# Aetherion Online — Milestone 11: Auto Hunting (with bulk sale, sorting and player trading)

Version **0.12.0-alpha**. Also in this pass: the monster spawn and navigation fix ([monster-navigation.md](monster-navigation.md)), a harder combat track and richer skill effects (section 8), and M12 ([M12.md](M12.md)).

## 1. Honest summary

- **Auto hunting works in the real multiplayer game.** In the browser test a character is started from the panel and kills every monster in its hunting area by real, server-resolved attacks, walks to the one out of reach, leaves alone the one outside the radius, drinks a potion at its threshold, and stops the instant Stop is pressed or a movement key is touched.
- **How it is built, and why that keeps the server in charge:** the hunter runs in the client and plays through exactly the commands a keyboard sends (move, target, attack, use ability, use potion). The server validates each as it always has. Automation therefore has no power a player lacks, and there is no new channel to exploit. The server adds the session on top: kill, EXP, gold and item counts, the loot filter, and ending the session on death, zone change or disconnect.
- **Not built:** attacks do not require line of sight in this game, so the *server* does not refuse an attack through a tree. The hunter itself never attacks without line of sight and never targets what it cannot walk to. There are no PvP zones yet (M15), so "do not enter PvP zones" has nothing to check.
- **No energy, stamina or time limit** was introduced. The project's own test for that still passes.
- **Not judged by a person:** whether auto hunting *feels* right (pacing, target choice in a crowd) has only been checked by tests.

## 2. What the player gets

Press **V** (or the **Auto** button) for the panel.

| Control | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Start / Stop Auto Hunting | Stop is immediate. Any movement key also stops it. |
| Auto attack, Auto skill, Auto potion, Auto loot, Auto target, Fight elites | Independent switches. Bosses are never chosen automatically. |
| HP / MP potion thresholds | Default 60% and 30%, adjustable 5–95%. |
| Hunting radius | 4–30 tiles around the spot where hunting started; drawn on the ground as a dashed circle. |
| Skills | Every usable skill listed with: on/off, priority (move up/down), minimum MP, minimum enemies (area skills), HP threshold (heals and protective skills). |
| Loot | Everything / equipment only / Rare and above; ignore potions, materials or scrolls; stop or continue when the bag is full. |
| When hunting ends by itself | Stay, walk back to the start, or walk to town. |
| Status | Running/Off, what it is doing, current target, HP/MP, thresholds, selected skills, monsters defeated, EXP earned, items and gold collected, time. |

The configuration is saved with the character in PostgreSQL.

## 3. Behaviour, rule by rule

**Auto potion.** Used only below the threshold, only if not already full, only if a potion exists, only off cooldown. The server re-checks all of this (and refuses with "Already at full health", "Potions are on cooldown" or "Item not found"; nothing is consumed on a refusal). When the stock runs out a chat line says so; if the character is then below half the threshold, hunting stops.

**Auto attack.** Nearest valid monster; walks to it along a route over the navigation grid; attacks when in range *and* in line of sight; the server's attack interval paces the blows; a new target is picked when the old one dies or becomes invalid; the current target is kept while valid (no flitting). Players and NPCs cannot be targeted at all (only monsters can).

**Auto skill.** By the player's priority order. A skill is used only if enabled, learned or unlocked, off cooldown, affordable, above its MP threshold, in range, and worth it: Flame Burst needs three enemies by default, Healing Rain and Mend fire below their HP threshold, and a skill costing 20 or more is not spent on an ordinary monster below 20% health unless the player allows it. Movement skills, stealth, taunt and resurrection are never automated.

**Auto loot.** Loot in this game goes straight to the killer's bag under the existing ownership and party rules; there is nothing lying on the ground to steal. Auto loot is therefore a *filter* on what the hunter keeps from its own kills: items the rules exclude are left behind and counted. Gold is always taken. Boss and cache rewards are never filtered.

**Safety.** It only walks where a route exists; it stays inside the hunting radius (asserted in the browser test); stuck detection (no progress for a second) triggers a sideways recovery step and a new route, and after three failures the target is given up for 20 seconds; unreachable targets are skipped; it stops on death, on a lost connection, and on leaving the zone.

## 4. Bulk sale, sorting, trading (requested alongside)

- **Bulk sale** (bag → at a merchant → **Bulk sell…**): mark items by clicking, or with the quick buttons (Common gear, Common + Uncommon gear, Materials); the panel shows the count and total; selling takes two clicks, the second naming the count and price. The server sells all marked items in one step or none.
- **Sort** (bag → **Sort by…**): type, rarity, item level, value or name. Done on the server: partial stacks are merged and the new order is stored.
- **Trade** (click a player → **Trade**, or `/trade Name`): invitation, accept or decline, items and gold from both sides, both confirm, exchange. Safeguards:
  - both players online, alive, in the same room and within 240 units, from invitation to exchange, or the trade is cancelled;
  - **any change to either offer clears both confirmations**;
  - items on the table are locked (cannot be sold, used, equipped, upgraded or destroyed);
  - the exchange re-checks ownership, quantities, gold and bag space, then moves everything in one step;
  - both inventories and an audit row (`trade_log`) are written in **one database transaction**;
  - items are moved, never copied; a test checks gold and item totals are conserved and every item id stays unique.

## 8. Also changed in this pass

- **Combat music** (requested: more intense when a battle starts): the normal combat track was rewritten at 172 bpm with a kick on every beat, double-kick fills, sixteenth-note bass and strings, war drums and brass; the elite track was raised to 184 bpm; a **battle-start impact sound** plays the moment a fight begins. Both tracks re-verified as seamless loops. Still temporary placeholder audio, still unheard by anyone (see M10.md).
- **Skill effects** (requested: more spectacular): a second, additive layer over the existing pixel effects: glows, shockwaves, sparks and embers that arc and fall, light pillars for heals and buffs, glowing lightning with branches, a falling meteor with a closing shadow and debris, a dome for shields, blade crescents with a bright leading edge, hit sparks on every blow (a star on criticals), screen shake and flash for heavy impacts scaled by distance. Shake and flash are disabled when the operating system asks for reduced motion. Frame rate in the browser test was unchanged (60). Not judged by eye.

## 9. Remaining risks

- The hunter is client-side: a modified client could send the same commands faster, exactly as it could without auto hunting. The server's existing limits (input budget, attack interval, cooldowns, message rate) are what stop that, and they are tested.
- Route searches for "return to town" can be long; they are limited to 40,000 nodes and give up with "No route back" if that is not enough.
- Trade persistence is one transaction for the two inventories and the log, but the inventories are JSON snapshots per character, as everywhere else in this project.
