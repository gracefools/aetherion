# Aetherion Online — Milestone 14: Guilds and the Player Economy

Version **0.15.0-alpha** (M14 and M15 were finished in the same pass).

## 1. Honest summary

- **Guilds, the bank and the auction house work end to end.** Each is decided by the server, stored in PostgreSQL, and reachable from the game's interface. Shown by unit, integration (real database, real sockets) and browser tests.
- **Trading, bulk sale and inventory sorting** were the first part of M14 and were done in M11 (docs/M11.md section 4). They are unchanged.
- **Money safety:** every change that moves gold or items writes the bag, the bank and the change itself in **one database transaction**; if it fails, the server's memory is put back as it was. Two buyers clicking one listing at the same moment: exactly one gets it (tested with real concurrent requests).
- **Not judged by a person:** fee and tax levels, the guild experience curve, and how the panels feel to use.

| Where | What |

Members with rank, level, job and online status; invite by name (or from a player's frame); promote, demote, remove, hand over leadership; message of the day; guild bank (gold); guild log; leave; disband (type the guild's name to confirm; the guild bank must be empty) |

## 3. Rules

**Guild ranks:** Leader, Officer, Member, Recruit. Officers and the leader invite, remove lower ranks, set the message and withdraw from the guild bank; only the leader changes ranks, hands over leadership or disbands. Anyone can deposit. Capacity 20 members, +5 per guild level (up to level 10). Members' kills give the guild a tenth of their experience.

**Auction house:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Listing fee | 5% of the price per 24 hours (12 h: 2.5%, 48 h: 10%), minimum 1 gold, paid when listing, never refunded |
| Sales tax | 10% of the price, taken from the seller's proceeds |
| Configuration | `AUCTION_FEE_PCT`, `AUCTION_TAX_PCT`; the interface shows the server's actual rates |
| Limits | 20 active listings per character; price 1 to 100,000,000 |
| Proceeds | Wait with the banker until collected; so do items whose listing ended or was cancelled while the bag was full |
| Price check | A buy carries the price the buyer saw; if the row in the database differs, nothing happens |
| Expiry | Checked every 30 seconds; unsold items go back to the seller's claims |

**Race safety of a purchase:** the listing row is claimed with `UPDATE … SET status = 'sold' WHERE id = $1 AND status = 'active' AND expires_at > now() AND seller_id <> buyer RETURNING …`. Only one transaction can get the row back; the other finds nothing and the buyer is told the listing is gone. The buyer's gold and bag and the seller's claim are written in that same transaction. A per-player lock stops one player from running two money operations at once.

**Bug found by the tests:** withdrawing a non-stackable item from the bank created a copy with a new identity instead of moving the item. Fixed; the test checks the identity survives.

**Change made with the interface:** after listing, buying, cancelling or collecting, the client may send the query it is showing; the server runs it again once the change is stored, so the list on screen is never stale.

| Suite | Result |

Integration (real PostgreSQL and sockets):
- **Guilds**: founding cost and refusals (name, tag, level, gold, taken name); invitation, acceptance, ranks and what each may do; message of the day; guild bank in and out with permissions; leadership transfer; leave; remove; disband with the name check and empty bank; all of it in the database.
- **Auction house**: fee, search and filters, purchase, tax, collecting, cancelling, expiry, history, the listing limit.
- **The race**: two buyers for one listing at the same moment.
- **Bank**: items whole and in part, gold, the slot limit, refused in combat, the history, and persistence after logging out.

Browser: found a guild with an emblem; invite the second player, who accepts; promote; guild chat; message of the day seen by the other member; guild bank deposit and the log; the tag on the other player's view. At the banker: part of a stack in and out, gold in, history. Auction: list two of a stack (fee shown and charged), the buyer searches by name and buys with confirmation, the list refreshes, the seller collects the proceeds less tax and sees the sale in History.
