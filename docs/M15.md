# Aetherion Online — Milestone 15: PvP, Pets and Mounts

Version **0.15.0-alpha**.

## 1. Honest summary

- **PvP, duels, the arena, pets and mounts work end to end**: decided by the server, stored in PostgreSQL, and reachable from the interface. Shown by unit, integration and browser tests.
- **Every hit between players is resolved by the server**, with the same damage code as monsters, scaled to 60% for PvP. The client only chooses what a click means.
- **Mounts change speed through the same movement code** that walking uses, so collision is identical (tested against a wall).
- **Pet and mount art is original**, drawn in code: six pets and four mounts, two frames each.
- **Not judged by a person:** PvP balance between jobs, the 60% damage factor, the rating changes, and how the pets and mounts look.

## 2. What the player gets

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **PvP badge** (top right) | The rule where you stand: *Safe zone*, *Town · safe*, *Duels only*, *Open PvP*, *Arena* (with a Leave button) |
| **Attacking a player** | Where the rules allow it, clicking a player targets them like a monster (red name, target ring, "Player" on the target frame); attack and single-target skills work. Elsewhere a click selects them as an ally, as before |
| **Duels** | Click a player, then **Duel** (or `/duel Name`). The other player gets a prompt. A 3-second countdown, then the duel; it ends at 1 health: nobody dies. **Forfeit** button; it also ends if the two move too far apart or after two minutes. Announced to everyone nearby |
| **Arena** | The **Arena Master** in Eldoria (F): enter, leave, and the rankings. Inside: no monsters, every level may fight every level, your party is your team, the fallen return inside the arena a moment later. A victory pays 25 gold |
| **Rankings** | At the Arena Master by rating, kills, duels won or arena victories, with your own record; also `GET /api/pvp/rankings?by=…` |
| **Companions panel (P)** | Your pets (level, experience, bonus; summon, dismiss, rename, release with confirmation) and mounts (speed, required level; ride, dismount) |
| **Whistles and reins** | Pet and mount items are used from the bag (Use, or double-click) to add the companion to the collection |
| **In the world** | A summoned pet follows at its owner's side; a rider sits on the mount. Other players see both |

## 3. Rules

**Zones:**

| Zone | Rule |
|---|---|
| Eldoria, Verdant Wilds (capital and beginners) | Safe: no fighting between players |
| Ashen Wastes, Frostveil | Duels only |
| Dreadmoor, Astral Rift | Open PvP outside towns |
| Dungeons | Safe |
| The arena | Everyone, except your own party |

**Open PvP:** both players level 20 or higher, at most 10 levels apart, neither in town, not in the same party. A defeated player is protected for 60 seconds after returning. Beating the same player again within 10 minutes gives no honour and no rating. A kill gives 10 honour and an Elo rating change (K = 24, start 1000). Being knocked down in PvP loses nothing else.

**Abilities in PvP:** single-target attacks and spells. Area, field and movement skills do not hit players (this keeps PvP away from crowd effects that were built for monsters). Self-buffs work as usual.

**Pets:** six kinds, each raising one stat (attack, health, magic, defence, critical chance or dodge) while summoned, by a bonus that grows from level 1 to 20. A summoned pet gains 15% of the experience its owner earns. Pets never block movement or fight.

**Mounts:** four kinds, 30% to 50% faster, each with a level requirement (10, 20, 20, 30). You cannot mount in combat, in dungeons or in the arena; taking damage, attacking or casting puts you back on foot, and so does entering a dungeon. Nobody logs in riding.

**Where they come from:** pet whistles drop rarely from elite monsters (3%) and are sold by factions at Honored; reins are sold by factions at Revered and Exalted (M13 faction shops). Both can be traded and auctioned.

## 5. Known limits

- Players are targeted by clicking; **Tab** still cycles monsters only.
- The arena is one shared room on the ruins map, not matched teams or queues.
- PvP balance between the six jobs has not been tuned.
- The auto hunter never attacks players.
