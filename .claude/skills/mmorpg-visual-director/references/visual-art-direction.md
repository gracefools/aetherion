# Visual Art Direction

## Objective
Establish one cohesive visual language for the entire MMORPG. The supplied reference image is a quality target for finish and atmosphere, not a requirement to copy its exact content.

## Standards
- Maintain a consistent pixel grid and intentional pixel clusters.
- Use readable silhouettes and controlled detail at the game's actual camera zoom.
- Define palette ramps for each material and environment.
- Separate foreground, playable space, background, and interactable objects through value and contrast.
- Use highlights and shadows consistently with the scene's light direction.
- Reserve the strongest contrast and brightest effects for gameplay-critical elements.
- Ensure characters remain distinguishable from terrain and effects.

## Style consistency checklist
- Consistent pixel scale across characters, props, tiles, and effects.
- Consistent outline weight and corner treatment.
- Harmonized saturation and hue relationships.
- Material-specific shading rather than generic gradients.
- No unintended blur, fractional scaling artifacts, or noisy dithering.
- UI and world art feel related without sacrificing legibility.

## Art bible deliverables
For a substantial visual overhaul, document palette swatches, outline rules, light direction, pixel scale, material ramps, sprite scale, and representative examples before producing large asset batches.
