Style B: HD-2D village

The chibi pixel sprites from style A, standing in a real 3D village. The camera tilts and turns, the sun casts shadows, lamps and windows light the street at dusk, and the pond reflects everything around it. Bloom makes light glow, and tilt-shift focus softens the far and near edges like a miniature. Everything is drawn by code; the 3D runs on WebGL.

WASD or tap the ground to move · Space attack · F talk · QE turn the camera
